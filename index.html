What Can You Do?

NukeRoleplay is more than just building and exploring. Create your own story and decide how you want to live.

🏠

Buy & Own Properties

Buy your own house, apartment or other properties. Build your home, customize it and give trusted players access to your property.

💼

Jobs & Careers

Choose your career and earn money through different jobs. Work your way up and build your own financial future.

💰

Legal & Illegal Economy

Make money legally through jobs and businesses — or take the risky path and earn money through illegal activities.

🚗

Vehicles & Transportation

Travel across the world using cars, public transportation and other ways to get around the cities.

👮

Police & Law

Follow the law, become a police officer, investigate crimes or live life on the other side of the law.

🌎

A Growing World

Explore cities, mountains, beaches and unique locations in a constantly expanding world filled with new places to discover.
