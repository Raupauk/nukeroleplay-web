The server

Make your own life.

NukeRoleplay isn't built around one way to play. Buy a place to live, find a job, start a business, join the police or simply explore the world.

What happens next is mostly up to you.

🏠

Properties

Buy houses, apartments and other properties. Build your home and give people you trust access to it.

💼

Jobs

Work different jobs, earn money and slowly build up your character's life.

💰

Economy

Make money legally through work and businesses — or take the less respectable route.

🚗

Vehicles

Get around the cities with cars, public transport and other vehicles.

👮

Police

Join the police, enforce the law, investigate crimes or find yourself on the other side of it.

🌎

The World

Cities, beaches, mountains and plenty of places to discover as the map continues to grow.
