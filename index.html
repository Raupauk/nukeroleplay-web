The server

Make your own life.

Every character in Oblivion City picks a lane. A landlord, a mechanic, a beat cop, a name the police keep a file on — nobody hands you a story here. You build it shift by shift.

DISTRICT · 01

Properties

Buy a house, an apartment, or a storefront downtown. Furnish it, lock it, hand out keys to people you actually trust.

FILE · 02

Jobs

Clock into a real shift and get paid for it. Grind long enough and it stops being pocket money.

FILE · 03

Economy

Earn it clean through work, or earn it fast the other way. Both routes leave a paper trail eventually.

DISTRICT · 04

Vehicles
