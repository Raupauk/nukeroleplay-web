Government / Overview

Power to
the people.

NukeRoleplay is building a player-driven government system. Instead of laws simply being handed down, players themselves will get the chance to represent the city, sit in Parliament and even become President.

The system is built around three pillars: a Parliament elected by the community, a President chosen by that Parliament, and a set of appointed positions and ministries that keep the city running.

Status · In Development

The government system doesn't exist in the game yet — this page describes the plan for how it will work. Details may still change before it goes live, so check back as development continues.

Parliament

A group of players elected by the community to represent them and propose new laws.

President

Elected by Parliament, the President leads the city and appoints its key positions.

Cabinet

Appointed roles such as the Chief of Police, Attorney General and various ministers.

Legislation

Parliament can propose changes to the law, within limits set by the server itself.

Government / Legislature

The
Parliament.

Parliament is the elected voice of the community. Its members are regular players, voted in by other players, who take on the responsibility of representing the city and shaping its laws.

Eligibility

Players need a certain amount of playtime on the server before they can run for a seat in Parliament.

Elections

Seats are filled during dedicated voting phases, held periodically for the community.

Representation

Members are chosen directly by the players who vote for them.

Responsibility

Parliament drafts and votes on proposals for new or changed laws.

Government / Voting

Voting
Phases.

Seats in Parliament aren't permanent. From time to time, a voting phase opens up and gives the community the chance to elect a new Parliament.

STEP · 01

Voting Phase Opens

A voting phase is announced, opening up Parliament seats for the community.

STEP · 02

Players Run for a Seat

Any player who meets the required playtime can put themselves forward as a candidate.

STEP · 03

The Community Votes

Players vote for the candidates they want to see representing them in Parliament.

STEP · 04

Parliament is Seated

Elected members take their seats and begin representing the players who voted for them.

Government / Executive

The
President.

Once a Parliament is seated, its first big decision is choosing who leads the city. Parliament votes for the President, who then takes on the executive role for the city of Oblivion.

The President doesn't govern alone. One of their main responsibilities is appointing key positions within the city's government — filling the leadership roles that keep departments and services running.

Executive · Limits

The President's power isn't unlimited. Changes to positions or laws still require approval from the Nuke Studios team, see Checks & Approval .

Government / Appointments

Cabinet &
Appointments.

After being elected, the President appoints players to lead some of the city's most important positions. These appointments are expected to grow over time as the government system develops further.

APPOINTMENT · 01

Chief of Police

Leads the Oblivion City Police Department and oversees its operations at the highest level.

APPOINTMENT · 02

Supreme Court Chief Justice

Heads the judicial side of the city's government and oversees how the law is interpreted.

APPOINTMENT · 03

Attorney General

Represents the government in legal matters and oversees how justice is carried out in the city.

APPOINTMENT · 04

Ministers

Cabinet ministers, such as a Health Minister or Education Minister, oversee specific areas of the city.

The exact list of ministries is still being worked out. As the government system grows, more appointed positions may be added to cover other areas of city life.

Government / Legislation

Making
the law.

One of Parliament's core jobs is proposing new laws and changes to the city's existing legal system. But a proposal from Parliament is only the first step.

STEP · 01

Parliament Proposes

A new law or an amendment to an existing one is drafted and voted on within Parliament.

STEP · 02

Presidential Approval

The President reviews the proposal and must approve it before it can move forward.

STEP · 03

Nuke Studios Review

The Nuke Studios team reviews the proposal to make sure it fits the server's roleplay.

STEP · 04

Law Enacted

Once approved by both the President and Nuke Studios, the change officially becomes law.

Government / Oversight

Checks &
Approval.

Parliament has real power, but that power isn't unlimited. Not everything about the city's rules and systems can be changed through the government alone.

Government · Oversight

Any change made by Parliament always requires approval from the President and from the Nuke Studios team, the staff involved in the server's roleplay. This keeps the government fun and meaningful without letting it break the server.

In short: players get real influence over how the city is run, while Nuke Studios keeps a final say to make sure everything stays fair, balanced and fun for everyone.
