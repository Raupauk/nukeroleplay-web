Jobs

Find your way into the workforce of NukeRoleplay. From simple side jobs to careers that require applications, certifications and responsibility.

Jobs / Overview

Find your
living.

Jobs are one of the most important ways to build a stable income in NukeRoleplay. Whether you want something simple to do whenever you need money or want to build an actual career inside the city, there are many different paths available.

The job system is split into two major categories: jobs without an application and jobs that require an application.

No Application

Simple jobs that anyone can start. Buy the required tool, go to work and sell what you collect.

Application Required

Career-based jobs that require you to join the associated Discord and complete an application process.

Build Your Career

Some jobs offer departments, certifications, ranks or additional responsibilities as you progress.

Make It Your Own

You are not limited to one way of making money. Combine different jobs and activities to build your own source of income.

Jobs / Employment

Work for
yourself.

Not every career in NukeRoleplay has to be tied to one of the predefined jobs. Players can also create their own companies and build their own businesses within the city.

Whether you want to run a service, create a construction company, operate a shop or come up with something completely different, starting your own company gives you the freedom to create your own role in the economy.

Create a Company

Build your own business and create a company around an idea, service or profession.

Hire Players

Grow your company by employing other players and giving them a way to earn money through your business.

Work for Others

You can also work for player-owned companies instead of running your own. Find a business that fits your interests and become part of its team.

Build Your Reputation

Player-run companies can become an important part of the city's economy. Good service and reliable employees can help a company grow over time.

Company creation, management and the different possibilities available to business owners are covered in more detail on the Businesses Documentation page.

Jobs / Open Employment

Start
working.

Application-free jobs are designed to be accessible to everyone. Once you have the necessary equipment, you can simply go to the relevant location and begin working.

The resources you collect can generally be sold again, giving you a straightforward way to turn your time into money.

JOB · 01

Miner

Head into the mines and extract ores and other blocks. The mines contain several different levels with different materials and challenges.

No Application
JOB · 02

Lumberjack

Enjoy the forest air while cutting wood and helping keep the forests clean. Collected wood can be sold or processed into other materials.

No Application
JOB · 03

Fisher

Travel to different biomes and fish for a variety of catches. Different locations can provide different things to bring home and sell.

No Application
JOB · 04

Junk Collector

The simplest job in the city. Walk through the streets, collect junk and sell it to collectors or Junk Shops. You can also use junk as materials to create completely new items.

No Application
JOB · 05

Delivery

Visit a CTT Station and choose a delivery route. Pick between Short, Medium or Long Deliveries and transport packages to different destinations across the world.

No Application
Miner · Environmental Hazard

The lower levels of the mines can be toxic. Do not assume that going deeper is always safer or more profitable. Prepare yourself before entering dangerous areas.

Job / Mining

Beneath
the city.

Mining is a straightforward way to turn your time into resources and money. Enter the mines, equip the appropriate tool and begin extracting ores and other blocks.

The mines are divided into multiple levels. Deeper levels can provide access to different resources, but they also become more dangerous.

Multiple Levels

Explore different depths of the mines to find different resources.

Tools

Purchase the appropriate equipment before entering the mines.

Sell Your Resources

Mined materials can be turned back into money through the appropriate selling or processing systems.

Watch the Depth

Lower mine levels can contain toxic environments. Know what you are getting into before going deeper.

Jobs / Resources

Work the
world.

Not every job takes place in a building. Some of the most accessible work in NukeRoleplay takes you directly into the world around you.

RESOURCE · 01

Lumberjack

Harvest wood from the forests. The materials can be sold directly or processed further into useful products.

RESOURCE · 02

Fisher

Fish across different biomes and discover that different waters can offer different catches.

RESOURCE · 03

Junk Collector

Search the streets for discarded items. Sell them for quick cash or keep the junk for crafting and other uses.

RESOURCE · 04

Processing

Some resources are more valuable when processed. Keep an eye out for opportunities to turn basic materials into something more useful.

Job / Delivery

Move it
around.

Delivery is an accessible job that lets you earn money by transporting packages to different destinations throughout the world. No application is required to get started.

Simply visit a CTT Station and choose the type of delivery you want to take. The available routes are divided into three different lengths.

Short Delivery

Short routes are ideal for getting started or completing quick delivery runs without spending too much time on the road.

Medium Delivery

Medium routes cover more distance and require a longer trip before the package reaches its destination.

Long Delivery

Long deliveries take you across larger parts of the world and are suited for players looking for a longer delivery run.

CTT Stations

CTT Stations are the starting point for delivery work. Visit one whenever you want to begin a new delivery.

Delivery · Choose Your Route

Consider how much time you have available before choosing a route. Short, Medium and Long Deliveries all require different amounts of travel.

An application job generally requires you to join the relevant Discord server and submit an application or form. Each organisation can have its own requirements and selection process.

Career / Police

Serve &
protect.

The Oblivion City Police Department is one of the most structured careers in NukeRoleplay. Officers enforce the law, respond to incidents and help keep the city safe.

Police officers can progress through ranks and may join specialised divisions such as SWAT, Aviation Support Unit, K-9 Corps or Internal Affairs.

Shift Officers

The operational officers who work shifts and handle day-to-day policing throughout the city.

Commanders

Commanders lead individual divisions and oversee their members and operations.

Chief Ranks

The highest ranks within the OCPD, responsible for leading the department as a whole.

Specialised Divisions

SWAT, ASU, K-9 Corps and Internal Affairs provide additional paths within the police department.

Police Application

Join the OCPD

Police applicants must complete the required process before joining. Visit the Police documentation for more information about the LETC, testing, ranks and divisions.

Career / Civil Management

Keep the
city alive.

Civil Management is responsible for keeping infrastructure around the city operational. When something breaks, Civil Management helps get it working again.

SERVICE · 01

ATMs

Repair damaged or broken ATMs so residents can continue to access essential financial services.

SERVICE · 02

Streetlights

Maintain streetlights and other public infrastructure around the city.

SERVICE · 03

Potholes

Repair damaged roads and help keep the city's streets usable.

SERVICE · 04

Metro Repairs

Help restore broken metro systems and other infrastructure that residents rely on.

Career / Justice

The law
works both ways.

The legal system provides two distinct career paths: representing people as a Lawyer or overseeing proceedings as a Judge.

LEGAL · 01

Lawyer

Become a lawyer by obtaining the required certification at the Law University. Once qualified, you can represent clients according to the rules of the legal system.

Law University
LEGAL · 02

Judge

Apply to the Department of Justice to become a judge. In the courtroom, judges oversee proceedings and make decisions on cases.

Department of Justice
Certification

Career / Construction

Build the
city.

ENGE Constructions gives qualified players the opportunity to contribute directly to the physical world of NukeRoleplay.

To become part of the team, you need an Architecture Degree from the University of Architecture. Once qualified, you can design houses and other structures and submit them through the discord server.

STEP · 01

Study Architecture

Obtain your Architecture Degree from the University of Architecture.

STEP · 02

Design

Create houses or other structures that could fit into the NukeRoleplay world.

STEP · 03

Submit

Submit your work through the required form. Approved creations may make their way onto the actual map.

The Nuke Studios Building Team also hosts regular training sessions and further education for members who want to improve their building and architectural skills.

Jobs / Classified

Not every
job is legal.

The jobs listed on this page are the legitimate careers and activities available to players through the city's normal systems.

There are also illegal ways to make money in NukeRoleplay. Those paths are intentionally not documented here.

Classified · Your Choice

If you decide to explore the criminal side of the city, remember that actions can have consequences. The police, courts and other systems of the city still apply.

Jobs / Application Guide

How to
apply.

Application-based jobs are managed by their respective organisations. The exact requirements can differ, but the general process is straightforward.

STEP · 01

Find the Organisation

Identify the department or company you want to join and find its official Discord server.

STEP · 02

Read the Requirements

Check the organisation's rules, requirements and application information before submitting anything.

STEP · 03

Apply

Complete the relevant form and follow the organisation's instructions for the next steps.

Important

Discord servers, application forms and requirements can change over time. Always follow the current information provided by the organisation itself.
