Progression / Overview

Grow your
character.

Your character grows in several ways: levels, reputation, licenses and degrees, and missions. This page shows how they fit together.

Level

Gain experience from jobs and activities and collect rewards at milestones.

Reputation

What the city thinks of you. It changes prices, interest and careers.

Licenses

Pay a fee, pass a test and unlock new options.

Missions

Optional tasks and discoveries.

Progression / Level & XP

Level
up.

Almost every job and many activities give you experience: mining, lumberjacking, fishing, farming, deliveries, daily quests and missions, and police work. Gaining enough experience raises your level.

At milestone levels you receive a money reward. Open the Level app on your phone to see your progress and collect your rewards. There are also rewards for the time you spend on the server.

Progression / Reputation

What they
think of you.

Your reputation is a measure of how the city sees you. Good deeds and clean behavior raise it, crime and violence lower it. It is shown as one of five tiers.

TierWhat changes
HonoredHighest bank interest, cheapest life insurance, 10% off in shops at the very top
RespectedGood interest and insurance rates
NeutralNormal rates. Required for police and lawyer careers
ShadyLower interest, more expensive life insurance
CriminalLowest interest, most expensive life insurance, 10% surcharge in shops and no property purchases at the very bottom

Wanted points also fade faster with a high reputation. Passing a license test gives a small reputation boost. See Economy for the exact numbers on interest and insurance.

Progression / Licenses & degrees

Licenses
and degrees.

Licenses and degrees cost money, require you to pass a test, and unlock things that are otherwise closed to you. You can see yours in the Degrees app.

LicenseCostUnlocks
Driving license5,000€Spawning and driving vehicles
Gun license15,000€Buying and carrying firearms
Basic Geology5,000€Lower mine levels (with a hazmat suit)
Advanced Geology20,000€Oil pumps
LETC50,000€Police career, see Police
Lawyer Certification50,000€Lawyer career, see Law & Order

Some licenses, like the hunting license, are bought and tested at special NPCs. Look around the city to find them.

Progression / Missions

Missions
and discoveries.

Use /missions to see your active missions. Missions are optional and take you around the city. They are meant to be discovered, so this documentation does not list them.

Progression / Advancements

Advance-
ments.

Advancements track what you have done in the city. They are grouped into eight tabs: General, Jobs, Business, Criminal, Law, Education, Property and City. Open the advancements screen to see which ones you have completed.
