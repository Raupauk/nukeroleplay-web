Phone

Your mobile connection to the city. Access communication, services, entertainment, information and more directly from your phone.

Phone / Overview

Your city
in your hand.

The NukeRoleplay phone is your central interface for many of the services and systems available throughout the city.

Depending on the phone you own, you can access applications for communication, real estate, jobs, emergency services, entertainment and many other features.

The NukeRoleplay Mobile Phone

Mobile Interface

Access your available applications through the in-game phone interface.

Multiple Apps

Different applications provide access to different server systems.

Phone Quality

Some phones provide access to more applications than others.

Special Access

Certain applications only appear when specific requirements are met.

Phone / Access

Open your
Phone

To access your phone, use a phone item that supports the NukeRoleplay mobile interface. Right-click the phone to open the application menu.

Supported phones can display a different selection of applications depending on the type of phone you own.

Important · Phone Types

Not every phone provides access to the same applications. A lower-tier or special phone may have fewer available applications.

Phone / Real Estate

Scotex
Real Estate

The Scotex application acts as your mobile real estate agent.

Use it to browse available properties and find a house or other property that fits your needs.

What can you do?

Purchase or rent properties directly through the real estate system. For more information about property ownership, rental payments, taxes and building, visit the Housing documentation.

Phone / Police

OCPD
Mobile Data

Police players can receive access to the OCPD Mobile Data Terminal.

The application provides access to relevant and confidential police data from wherever the officer is located.

Restricted Application

The Mobile Data Terminal is only displayed when the player is recognized as a member of the police system.

Confidential

Police data can contain confidential information. Access to the application is therefore restricted to eligible police players.

Phone / Exploration

Discover
the World

The Discoveries application allows you to review locations you have discovered throughout the world.

The application becomes available when you have progressed far enough in the relevant discovery mission.

Phone / Entertainment

Trivolio
Theater

Players with the required permissions for the Trivolio Theater can access the Trivolio App.

The application provides tools to manage shows at the Trivolio Theater.

Permission Required

The application only appears for players who are show hosts or have been trusted by the current show host.

Phone / Upgrades

Upgrade
Your Phone

Not every phone has access to every application.

The junk phone, for example, provides a more limited selection of apps. Additional applications can become available by purchasing a better phone.

Limited Device

If an application is missing from your phone, it may simply be unavailable on your current device rather than being disabled for yourself.

Junk Phone

Provides a reduced application selection and displays information encouraging players to upgrade.

Better Phones

Purchasing a better phone can unlock additional applications and services.
