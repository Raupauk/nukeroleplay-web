Police / Overview

Serve the
city.

The Oblivion City Police Department, commonly known as the OCPD, is responsible for protecting the people of Oblivion City and enforcing the law.

Police is one of the most complex jobs in NukeRoleplay. Officers have access to specialized tools, departments and systems that allow them to handle everything from everyday patrols to major incidents.

Officers are expected to act professionally, follow the law and use their authority responsibly. Good work can lead to promotions and eventually to positions of leadership within the department.

OCPD

The Oblivion City Police Department protects the city and enforces its laws.

Career

Officers can progress through the department by demonstrating good work and responsibility.

Specialized Units

Officers can become part of specialized departments such as SWAT, ASU, K-9 Corps and Internal Affairs.

Law Enforcement

Police officers are expected to enforce the law while acting professionally and responsibly.

Police / Recruitment

Join the
OCPD.

Becoming a police officer is not as simple as selecting the job. Players must first demonstrate that they understand the responsibilities that come with being a member of law enforcement.

STEP · 01

Visit the LETC

Before joining the police, players must visit the Law Enforcement Training Center and take the required test.

STEP · 02

Pass the Test

The LETC test consists of multiple-choice questions. You must pass the test before continuing with the recruitment process.

STEP · 03

Contact the OCPD

After passing the LETC test, check the OCPD Discord for information about how to officially apply and enroll.

→ OCPD Discord
Police / Training

LETC

The Law Enforcement Training Center, or LETC, is where aspiring officers prove that they understand the fundamentals of law enforcement before joining the OCPD.

Law Enforcement Training Center · LETC

The LETC is not only useful for new recruits. Current police officers can return to the training center whenever they want to practice and improve their skills.

Recruitment Test

New applicants must complete the multiple-choice LETC test before they can proceed with recruitment.

Officer Training

Existing officers can use the LETC to train and practice at any time.

Police / Career

Rise through
the ranks.

The OCPD has a structured rank system. Officers can progress through the department by doing good work, gaining experience and taking on greater responsibilities.

01

Shift Officers

The frontline officers of the OCPD. Shift Officers handle everyday police work, patrols, calls and enforcement.

02

Commanders

Commanders are responsible for leading divisions and overseeing the officers working within them.

03

Chief Ranks

The highest ranks in the OCPD. Chief Ranks oversee the department as a whole and provide overall leadership for the police force.

Career · Responsibility

Higher ranks come with greater responsibility. Officers should always remember that rank is earned through good work and responsible conduct.

Police / Departments

Specialized
Units.

The OCPD is divided into several specialized departments. Each unit focuses on a different area of police work and can have its own members and progression.

UNIT · 01

SWAT

A specialized tactical unit for situations that require a higher level of preparation, coordination and equipment.

UNIT · 02

ASU

The Aviation Support Unit provides aerial support for police operations and situations where an elevated view is useful.

UNIT · 03

K-9 Corps

The K-9 Corps specializes in police work involving trained police dogs and their handlers.

UNIT · 04

Internal Affairs

Internal Affairs focuses on maintaining accountability and investigating issues involving members of the department.

Officers can become members of specialized units and progress through their respective structures. Commanders are responsible for leading divisions, while the Chief Ranks oversee the department at the highest level.

Police / Enforcement

Wanted
List.

Police stations provide an important service to everyone in the city. At the reception desk, any player can view the current list of wanted players.

Police station · Wanted player information

The wanted list allows players and officers to see which individuals are currently wanted by law enforcement.

Law Enforcement

Being wanted can have serious consequences. Police officers are responsible for handling wanted players according to the applicable laws and procedures.

Police / Equipment

Tools of
the trade.

Police officers have access to a variety of tools and systems designed to make their work more efficient. These tools allow officers to access relevant information, respond to situations and perform their duties throughout the city.

Mobile Data

Officers can access relevant police information through specialized police systems while away from a station.

Police Equipment

Specialized equipment helps officers handle different situations and perform their duties efficiently.

Communication

Police work depends on effective communication between officers, divisions and leadership.

Training

Officers can continue practicing and improving their skills at the LETC.

Police / Conduct

Enforce
the law.

Police officers have a position of authority and are expected to use that authority responsibly.

Every officer should act according to the law, treat civilians fairly and avoid unnecessary use of their authority. Professional conduct is an important part of being a member of the OCPD.

OCPD Standard

Police officers must always act within the law. Misusing police authority can damage public trust and may have consequences for an officer's career.

The goal of the OCPD is not simply to punish criminals. Officers are there to maintain order, protect civilians and keep Oblivion City safe.
