Police / Overview

Serve the
city.

The Oblivion City Police Department, commonly known as the OCPD, is responsible for protecting the people of Oblivion City and enforcing the law.

Police is one of the most complex jobs in NukeRoleplay. Officers have access to specialized tools, departments and systems that allow them to handle everything from everyday patrols to major incidents.

Officers are expected to act professionally, follow the law and use their authority responsibly. Good work can lead to promotions and eventually to positions of leadership within the department.

OCPD

The Oblivion City Police Department protects the city and enforces its laws.

Career

Officers earn Police XP and rise through the ranks automatically.

Specialized Units

Higher ranks unlock specialized teams such as SWAT, K-9 and the Aviation Support Unit.

Law Enforcement

Police officers are expected to enforce the law while acting professionally and responsibly.

Police / Joining

Join the
OCPD.

There is no application. Anyone who meets the requirements can become a police officer.

STEP · 01

Get the LETC License

Take the exam at the Law Enforcement Training Center. The license costs 3,000€.

STEP · 02

Check your reputation

You need a reputation of at least 80, which is Neutral.

STEP · 03

Start your shift

Open your phone and choose Job Selector → Oblivion City Police Department → Shift Officer. You are enrolled automatically as a Rookie.

Locked

Without the LETC License the entry is locked. It shows a red ✘ and "Requires: LETC License".

Police / Training

LETC

The Law Enforcement Training Center, or LETC, is where aspiring officers prove that they understand the fundamentals of law enforcement before joining the OCPD.

Law Enforcement Training Center · LETC

The LETC is not only useful for new recruits. Current police officers can return to the training center whenever they want to practice and improve their skills.

License Exam

The exam has 6 multiple-choice questions and you can get at most 2 wrong. All questions are about what officers may and may not do, for example consequences of crimes on duty, who may be handcuffed, lethal force, de-escalation and what to do after cuffing.

Officer Training

Existing officers can use the LETC to train and practice at any time.

Police / Career

Rise through
the ranks.

Ranks up to Commander are earned automatically with Police XP. On promotion you get a title and a message, and if you are on duty your new tools are unlocked immediately. Locked teams and actions show a red ✘ and the rank you need.

Earning XP

  • Arrest: 15 XP plus 10 XP per wanted star of the suspect
  • Citation: 5 XP
  • Killing a wanted player: 4 XP per wanted star
  • You get no XP if the suspect was not wanted.
  • You only get XP for the same suspect and the same action once per 60 minutes.

Deputy Chiefs and Chief

Ranks above Commander cannot be earned. The Chief of Police is set by the government or by admins. The Chief chooses up to 2 Deputy Chiefs, only from players who are Commander. Deputy Chiefs and the Chief earn no XP.

Personnel

Officers cannot be promoted, demoted, added or removed by other officers, not even by Captains and above. Ranks only change through XP, appointments and admins.

Police / Departments

Specialized
Units.

SWAT, K-9 and the Aviation Support Unit are teams that you unlock with your police rank. They do not have ranks of their own. Every team keeps its own equipment.

UNIT · 01

SWAT

A specialized tactical unit for situations that require a higher level of preparation, coordination and equipment. Unlocked at Detective.

UNIT · 02

K-9

Police work with trained police dogs and their handlers. Unlocked at Sergeant.

UNIT · 03

ASU

The Aviation Support Unit provides aerial support for police operations. Unlocked at Deputy Commander.

Police / Enforcement

Wanted
List.

Police stations provide an important service to everyone in the city. At the reception desk, any player can view the current list of wanted players. Officers can only cuff players who are wanted. From Lieutenant, officers may also cuff players without a wanted level.

How wanted stars build up, what happens when you are caught and how fines work is explained from a citizen's point of view on the Law & Order page.

Police / Equipment

Tools of
the trade.

Rank unlocks your equipment, see the table above. Items with the team tool tag, such as your uniform, weapons, handcuffs and ID, cannot be dropped, put into chests or other containers, item frames or armor stands.

Handcuffs

Cuff wanted players, search them and check their degrees.

Mobile Data

The MFD shows your rank, XP and next rank in Police Career, with the full list of ranks and perks. The Player Database shows the crimes recorded for a suspect.

Citations

From Police Officer. Choose a recorded crime that has a fine. The fine is charged and the crime is removed from the record.

Arrests

From Constable. One button sends the suspect to prison for the combined sentence of all recorded crimes, at most 30 minutes.

Officers never type reasons, fines or sentences. The system records the crimes it detected and you choose from them. A citation clears the wanted level once no crimes are left, an arrest clears all recorded crimes. If a player is wanted but no specific crime is recorded, Disturbing the Peace is used. Logging out while cuffed counts as Escape from Custody and raises the wanted level.

Police / Conduct

Enforce
the law.

Police officers have a position of authority and are expected to use that authority responsibly.

Every officer should act according to the law, treat civilians fairly and avoid unnecessary use of their authority. Professional conduct is an important part of being a member of the OCPD.

OCPD Standard

Police officers must always act within the law. Misusing police authority can damage public trust and may have consequences for an officer's career.

The goal of the OCPD is not simply to punish criminals. Officers are there to maintain order, protect civilians and keep Oblivion City safe.

Officers cannot commit crimes

If you commit a crime while in a police team, you are moved to the Civilian team immediately, lose all team tools and are suspended for 3 minutes per penalty point, at least 30 and at most 180 minutes. All police teams show "Suspended for X" in the Job Selector. Everything that gives a normal player a wanted level counts, and so does shooting, hitting or killing players without a wanted level, civilian NPCs or police NPCs. The Fight Club region is not affected.
