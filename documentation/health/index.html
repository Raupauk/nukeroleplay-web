Health System / Overview

Stay
alive.

Fights, bullets, falls and accidents can hurt you. The health system tracks injuries on different parts of your body, bleeding, illnesses and what happens when you go down. This page explains how to treat yourself, how to help others and how the hospital works.

Injuries

Six body zones, seven injury types, three grades each.

First aid

Plasters, bandages and medicine from the pharmacy.

Hospital

Doctors, treatment rooms and emergency calls.

Insurance

Health insurance pays for your treatment.

Use /health at any time to open your body menu and see your current injuries.

Health System / Injuries

Hurt
and healing.

You can get hurt in six body zones: head, torso, left and right arm and left and right leg.

Injury types

Bruise, laceration, gunshot wound, sprain, fracture, burn and concussion. Every injury has one of three grades: minor, moderate or severe.

How you get hurt

Injuries come from fights, arrows, guns, falls, explosions, fire and lava, and from being run over by vehicles.

What injuries do

Body partEffect
LegsYou move slower. Severe leg injuries stop you from sprinting and jumping.
ArmsMining and attacks get weaker. Severe arm injuries stop you from using a bow, and you may drop your item.
TorsoYour maximum health is lower.
HeadNausea and darkness.

Healing

Injuries heal on their own, but slowly: from a few minutes up to an hour and a half, depending on the injury. Treatment makes them heal much faster. Treatment at the hospital is the fastest and takes a few minutes at most. First aid items are in between.

Health System / Bleeding

Stop the
bleeding.

Some injuries make you bleed. Bleeding drains your health over time, but it never kills you by itself. Light bleeding stops on its own after about 90 seconds. Stronger bleeding needs a bandage.

ItemStops
PlasterLight bleeding
BandageLight and moderate bleeding
Pressure bandageAny bleeding
Health System / Going down

Down but
not out.

A fatal hit does not kill you instantly. You go down for 3 minutes. While you are down you can only crawl very slowly, your screen is dark, and you cannot attack, use items, drop things or run most commands. Nearby players get an alert with your coordinates.

Reviving a player

Anyone can revive a downed player.

  • CPR: right click the downed player with an empty hand again and again until the bar is full.
  • AED: hold the AED, right click the downed player and stay close for a few seconds.

Revived players come back with a little health and keep their injuries.

Giving up

You can give up by holding sneak for 5 seconds. If the timer runs out, you die.

Do not log out

If you log out while you are down, you die when you log back in.

Health System / Dying

The hospital
wakes you.

After you really die, you wake up in the nearest hospital and all your injuries and illnesses are gone.

What happens to your money depends on your life insurance, which is separate from the health insurance. Without it you lose half of your pocket money, with it only a small part. See Economy & Money.

Health System / First aid

Pharmacy
and first aid.

Medical items are sold in the pharmacy. Right click an item to use it on yourself, or right click another player to treat them. A progress bar fills while you stand still and keep the item in your hand.

Limits

Head and torso injuries cannot be treated on yourself.

Health System / Illnesses

Catch a
cold.

You can catch a cold, the flu, food poisoning, a stomach bug, a wound infection or a tapeworm. Sick players get symptoms: messages and effects like slowness, nausea or constant hunger.

Health System / Hospital

Going to
the hospital.

There are two hospitals: Master City and Campanha. Treatment is only available while a hospital staff member is online.

Getting treated

  • Go to the reception NPC and draw a ticket. Choose the reason: injured, ill, check-up, prescription or sick note, or something else.
  • Wait in the waiting area and stay inside the hospital. If you leave the hospital or wait longer than 15 minutes without being called, your ticket is cancelled.
  • When a doctor calls you, you get a message. Go to the treatment room and sit down on the doctor's chair.
  • The doctor can check you with the X-ray machine and the vital signs monitor, and then treats your injuries. Treatment costs money, see below.

Doctors can also write you a prescription and a sick note.

Health insurance

The health insurance NPC signs you up for free. It costs 50€ per day, taken automatically once a day. If you cannot pay for 3 days in a row, the insurance ends. It covers hospital treatment at the doctor's chair and your prescribed medicine.

The health insurance is separate from the life insurance, which only reduces what you lose when you die.

Paying for treatment

  • With health insurance you pay nothing.
  • Without insurance the money is taken right away.
  • If you cannot pay, you get an invoice in the Messenger app. Click the message to pay it.
Unpaid bills

Unpaid bills block hospital treatment after 24 hours, or earlier if your open bills reach 3,000€.

Health System / Prescriptions

Prescriptions
and notes.

A doctor (Medic rank or higher) can issue a prescription for one medicine. It is valid for 7 days and can be used once. Hand it to the pharmacy NPC by right clicking with the prescription in your hand.

  • With health insurance you get the medicine for free.
  • Without insurance you pay the normal price and keep the prescription.
  • You can also buy any medicine without a prescription. The prescription is just the cheaper way for insured players.

Sick notes

A sick note is a signed paper that says you are unfit for work for 1, 3, 7 or 14 days. It is only a roleplay document, the server does not enforce anything with it.

Health System / Emergency calls

Call
911.

Open your phone, use the Telephone app, dial 911 and choose Oblivion City Hospital Service. Describe your emergency in chat and the medical staff are notified.

If you are downed, an emergency call with your position is created automatically.

Tip

Call 911 when someone is down and nobody around can revive them.

Health System / Tips

Tips for
players.

  • Carry a bandage or two and a painkiller. They are cheap.
  • Do not eat raw or rotten meat if you can avoid it.
  • Stay out of the rain for long periods, or keep cold medicine with you.
  • If someone is down, start CPR immediately.
  • Get the health insurance before you need it. Treatments can be expensive.
