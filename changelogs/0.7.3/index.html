VERSION 0.7.3

Health & Underground

November 14, 2026
📋 Dev Blog — Update 0.7.3

Update 0.7.3 is here, and the biggest change is that getting hurt now actually matters. The new health system is live, alongside a new hospital, a big wave of underground and Mafia content, smarter and more lifelike NPCs, a round of police changes, and a lot of new shops.

Let's start with the health system 🩺. Fights, bullets, falls, explosions, fire and even being run over by a vehicle now injure specific parts of your body: your head, torso, arms and legs. Every injury has a type and a severity, and they come with real effects. Leg injuries slow you down, arm injuries weaken your attacks and your mining, torso injuries lower your maximum health, and head injuries bring nausea and darkness. Some injuries make you bleed, which drains your health over time until it stops on its own or you bandage it. Injuries heal by themselves, but slowly, so you'll want to treat them. Plasters, bandages, splints, painkillers and other medicine are sold at the pharmacy, and you can treat yourself or other players. Use /health at any time to see your current injuries.

You can also get sick 🤧. Standing in the rain for too long, eating raw meat, leaving a wound untreated or staying close to a sick player can all give you an illness, from a simple cold to food poisoning or a wound infection. Face masks 😷 make it harder to spread diseases, and the right medicine cures you.

A fatal hit doesn't kill you instantly anymore. Instead, you go down for a few minutes, unable to do much while the people around you decide whether to help. Other players can revive you with CPR or an AED, and if nobody can, someone can call 911 for the medical staff. If you do die, you wake up in the hospital.

Speaking of which, the new hospital 🏥 is the place to be when things get serious. Draw a ticket at the reception, wait for a doctor to call you, and let them treat you at the treatment room. Doctors can also write prescriptions and sick notes. Treatment costs money, but health insurance pays for it, so get covered before you need it.

Down in the underground 🕶️, there's a lot of new Mafia content, including drug dealers and more drugs to deal with, and it's now easier to get into the business. Ski masks hide your nametag while you wear them. On the law enforcement side 🚓, the police handcuff tool was remade, Lieutenants and above can now arrest players without them needing to be wanted, and the prison shows a bossbar with your remaining time. Committing a crime while working a job can now get you suspended from that job.

The world around you got more lifelike too 🧍. NPCs now react to more of the things happening around them, and they remember you, both the good and the bad. Police NPCs have also gotten a bit smarter, so you're better off not holding a weapon while one is nearby.

Our Discord bot 🤖 learned some new tricks as well: it now sends birthday reminders and has its own coin system, with commands like /fish to earn some coins.

On the shopping front 🛍️, the Icy Balls, Guci, H&N, Dennis Cloth and Backpack stores are open for business, and there's a Backpack Mechanic too. Most shops now stay closed at night, so plan your trips. New players are greeted by a starter guide NPC at spawn, and voting now rewards you with a Money Crate 🎁.

Finally, we adjusted a lot of numbers: mining XP, Delivery job and Daily Quest rewards, and the prices for degrees and licenses. And if you stir a cauldron with your bare hand, be careful: it burns 🔥.
