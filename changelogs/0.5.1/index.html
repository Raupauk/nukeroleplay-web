VERSION 0.5.1

World Expansion

August 8, 2026

🌍 World & Building

  • Added a new hospital on the other side of Sampaio.
  • Added Alpine Peaks, a new great mountain range with a castle, mountain lift and a church in the woods.
  • Added a large bridge connecting Sampaio with Alpine Peaks.
  • Added new buildings to Sampaio, including the Karl Cathedral and several skyscrapers.
  • Added new buildings and more nature to Ermesinde.
  • Added new buildings to Sintra.
  • Added a new high school to Fanzeres.
  • Added new student quarters and a hotel to Fanzeres.
  • Decorated Fanzeres beach.
  • Terraformed the oceans around the map borders.
  • Added yachts around the world. Players will be able to buy yachts and live on them, similar to houses.
  • Updated BlueMap.

⚙️ Systems & Mechanics

  • Added the Sintra Passport Official NPC.
  • Set up new houses in the housing system.
  • Fixed players being unable to build in house garages.
  • Fixed a bug in the Miner and Lumberjack jobs causing job blocks to stop working.
  • Added more telephone cells.
  • Changed the custom block system.
  • Fixed a bug in the ticket system causing incorrect permissions for users.
  • Fixed an issue with the Trivolio Theater causing shows to never start.
  • Added the first version of IceGP.
