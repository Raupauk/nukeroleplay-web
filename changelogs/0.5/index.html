VERSION 0.5

Jobs & Economy

July 9, 2026

🌍 World & Building

  • Finished the F1 Racing Track.
  • Added a new Spawn Area.
  • Added the White House Garden.
  • Added new buildings in Sintra.
  • Finished decorating most buildings in Sintra.
  • Updated BlueMap.
  • Added Björn the Castle Keeper.
  • Added Zandor's Magic Show in Grimwater.

💼 Jobs & Economy

  • Remade the Miner Job.
  • The new mines are located at the Master City Quarry instead of Dry Valley.
  • Mines now consist of two levels instead of three.
  • Mines are significantly larger and contain more ores and mineable blocks.
  • Fixed money checks not working correctly.
  • Fixed Lumberjack and Miner tools not losing durability.

🛍️ Shops & NPCs

  • Added Helena's Spawner Shop.
  • Added the Industrial Shop selling Ore Crushers, Industrial Crafters and more.
  • Added firework rockets with different designs to Travolios Stage Shop.
  • Redefined multiple NPC regions.

⚙️ Systems & Mechanics

  • Updated multiple plugins and outdated scripts.
  • Added XP caps to passive spawners, which increase with level.
  • Fixed passive spawners only using half of their storage at Storage Level 3.
  • Added the /loginstreak command, giving players a daily reward for staying active.
  • Remade the passport system.
  • Players can now set their hometown at the passport official of their preferred town.
  • Added the /house menu and /house trust commands.
  • Players can now trust others and give them permissions inside their houses.
  • Fixed several bugs within the new housing system.

📱 Mobile & Crafting

  • Changed Junk Phone functions by removing apps that are now limited to better phones.
  • Added Tweeter to the Mobile Phone.
  • Set up telephone cells.
  • Remade recipe menus for the Wood Bench Saw and Carpenter's Table.
  • Added the Ore Crusher.
  • Added blasting recipes for gold, iron and copper.
  • Added the Industrial Crafter.
  • Fixed material formatting in the Bench Saw and Carpenter's Table.
  • Fixed the Bench Saw and Carpenter's Table accepting Custom Items as materials.

👮 Police & Law

  • Added Grimwater Police Station as a spawn point for police teams.
  • Fixed a bug where fighting in the Fightclub activated a police call.

🎮 Gameplay & Events

  • Fixed a bug causing players in the Fightclub menu not to be displayed.
  • Fixed a bug where players could trust themselves as show hosts in the Trivolio Theater.

🐛 Bug Fixes

  • Fixed players being unable to select items from their inventory in the Auction House.
  • Changed iBuy History to show the Seller instead of the Buyer.
  • Fixed players being unable to add fuel to Oil Pumps.
  • Fixed mushroom pickup.
  • Fixed players being able to interact with armor stands without permission.
