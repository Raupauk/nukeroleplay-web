VERSION 0.5.2

Underworld & Expansion

August 21, 2026

🌍 World & Building

  • Added new buildings near the spawn in Ermesinde.
  • Furnished and completed the Bolon Market in Ermesinde.
  • Added a new hospital in North Sampaio.
  • Added new medium-density buildings in Fanzeres.
  • Added new apartment decorations in Sampaio.

📍 Spawn

  • Players now spawn at their last location instead of the world spawn.
  • Added spawn protection when joining.
  • Added no-spawn zones that kick players out when joining inside of them.
  • Added /spawn.
  • Added a new server spawn point in Ermesinde.
  • Added a new server IP.
  • Added the tutorial area to the spawn in Ermesinde.

💰 Economy & Shops

  • Added the Abimbas Sneaker Shop.
  • Added more NPC shops around the world.
  • Added housing taxes.
  • Added Vertex Properties, a company selling offices.
  • Added Royce the Pawnbroker.
  • Added ammunition to all guns.

🔫 Crime & Missions

  • Added the Undercity.
  • Added a new ATM Robbing System.
  • Added three new Mafia missions.
  • Added the mission "The Corrupt Cop".
  • Added Passcode Scanners for the Undercity.
  • Added Piranhas swimming in the sewers.

🎓 Housing & Jobs

  • Added Graduation Hats for players receiving a university degree.
  • Added new CTT delivery locations across the world.

🖥️ UI & Server

  • Updated the tab list.
  • Added new chat formatting.
  • Added new lore tags for items and applied them across the server.
  • Remade permissions for Players, Moderators and Team ranks.

🧱 Models & Props

  • Added new kitchen models, for example counters, cabinets, sinks and more in different variants.
  • Added new kitchen appliances, including Microwaves, Coffee machines, Blenders, Toasters, Fridges, Ovens, Stoves and more.
  • Added new interior and exterior props, such as Air conditioning units, Trash cans and bins, Computers and more.

🐛 Bug Fixes

  • Fixed the layout of car spawners.
  • Fixed players being able to vote multiple times during a votekick.
  • Fixed passive spawner upgrade items not working correctly.
  • Fixed players being able to take books from lecterns.
  • Fixed players in prison being able to use /spawn.
  • Fixed NPCs walking outside their designated areas.
  • Removed nametags from Fightclub NPCs.
