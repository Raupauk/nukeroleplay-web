VERSION 0.4.7

World Expansion

June 30, 2026

🌍 World & Building

  • Major terraforming on the eastern area of the map.
  • Added a new mountain range near Ermesinde.
  • Added the Twin River Bridge.
  • Finished the Main Government Building, the "White House", in Ermesinde.
  • Added the Emerald Villa near Sintra.
  • Finished decorating Grimwater.
  • Added Sintra, a new town with greenhouses, beaches and farms.
  • Added many new buildings in Sintra and a new tram system.
  • Added the National Bank in Sintra.
  • Added sand to the runway in Dry Valley.
  • Added the X-Mas City Train Station.
  • Decorated the Campanha Train Station.

💼 Jobs & Economy

  • Added the Wood Bench Saw.
  • Added the Carpenter's Table and Carpenter's Shop in the Lumber Yard.
  • Adjusted Lumberjack prices.
  • Added Lumber Price Boost based on the total amount of wood a player has farmed.
  • Civilian paychecks reduced from €350 to €120 per 10 minutes.
  • Fixed a bug in the Stock Market allowing players to sell non-existent shares.

🛍️ Shops & NPCs

  • Added Jono's Dye Shop with daily restocking.
  • Added Granny's Wool Shop.
  • Added Poppy's Flower Shop.
  • Added the IKAA Store.

👮 Police & Law

  • Wanted players can no longer use public transit.
  • Fixed several bugs in the handcuff and traffic cone tools.

⚙️ Systems & Mechanics

  • Reworked the Housing System.
  • Reworked sitting functions.
  • Reworked Level System rewards.
  • Added Passive Spawners.
  • Fixed several timed events.
  • Fixed vehicle plugin performance issues.
  • Fixed NPC movement behavior.
  • Removed sitting functions from NPCs.
