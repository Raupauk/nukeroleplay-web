Server Rules

These rules apply to everyone playing on the NukeRoleplay Minecraft server and to everyone in our Discord community. Please read them before you play. Not knowing the rules is not an excuse for breaking them.

1. Respectful Conduct

Treat every player and staff member with respect, both in-game and on Discord. This is a community built by and for its players, and everyone should feel comfortable being part of it.

Harassment, bullying, threats, insults and deliberately targeting or provoking other players will not be tolerated, regardless of whether it happens in chat, voice, roleplay or private messages.

2. No Discrimination or Hate Speech

Racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia and any other form of discrimination or hate speech based on someone's race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or any other personal characteristic is strictly forbidden.

This also applies fully during roleplay. "It's just my character" or "it's just roleplay" is never a valid excuse. Hateful symbols, slurs and discriminatory language are not allowed in any context, in character or out of character.

3. No Pornographic or NSFW Content

Pornographic, sexually explicit or otherwise NSFW content is not allowed anywhere on the server or in our Discord. This includes builds, skins, images, text roleplay, links and usernames.

Sexual or romantic roleplay involving minors, or content that sexualises minors in any way, is strictly forbidden and will result in an immediate and permanent ban.

4. Harassment, Threats & Doxxing

Do not share another person's private information, such as their real name, address, phone number, workplace or other identifying details, without their explicit consent.

Threats of violence, self-harm or any form of real-world harm directed at another player, whether serious or "as a joke", are taken seriously and will not be tolerated.

5. Cheating, Exploits & Unfair Advantage

Using cheat clients, hacked clients, X-ray, macros, scripts or any other third-party software that gives you an unfair advantage is forbidden.

Knowingly abusing bugs, glitches or exploits instead of reporting them to staff is also a rule violation, even if you did not cause the bug yourself.

6. Advertising & Spam

Advertising other Minecraft servers, Discord servers, social media pages or unrelated products and services is not allowed without staff permission.

Spamming chat, commands, join requests or pinging other members and staff unnecessarily is not allowed.

7. Account Security & Impersonation

You are responsible for the security of your own account. Sharing accounts, using another player's account or attempting to access an account that is not yours is not allowed.

Impersonating other players, staff members or the NukeRoleplay team, in-game, on Discord or elsewhere, is forbidden.

8. Staff Instructions & Moderation

Staff members are here to keep the server fair and enjoyable for everyone. Follow reasonable instructions given by staff, and treat moderation decisions with respect even if you disagree with them.

If you believe a decision was unfair, raise it calmly through the proper channels instead of arguing publicly or ignoring staff.

9. Enforcement

Breaking these rules can result in actions such as a warning, mute, kick, temporary ban or permanent ban, depending on the severity and context of the violation.

Staff members decide on the appropriate action on a case-by-case basis. Serious violations, such as hate speech, NSFW content involving minors or real-world threats, can lead to an immediate permanent ban without a prior warning.

These rules may be updated over time. Continued use of the server means you accept the current version of these rules.

10. Fictional Content

NukeRoleplay is a fictional roleplay setting. All NPCs, including their names, skins, dialogue and backstories, as well as all in-game events, storylines, organisations and locations, are fictional and created for entertainment purposes only.

Some NPCs are generated with randomised names and skins. Any resemblance to real persons, living or deceased, or to real organisations and events, is purely coincidental and not intended.

Report a Rule Violation

Seen someone breaking these rules? You can report a player at any time on our moderation Discord server, where our staff team will look into it.
